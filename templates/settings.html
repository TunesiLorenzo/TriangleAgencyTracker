SPEAKERS

Keep-alive drone

Amplifiers and Bluetooth speakers with auto-standby switch off when it goes quiet and cut the start of the next sound. From the first click on the viewer, a very quiet low drone plays continuously to keep them awake, and the first sound waits half a second while the speaker wakes up. Muting a viewer stops the drone too. Raise the drone volume if your speaker still falls asleep; lower it if you can hear a hum.

ROOM LIGHTS

LightRPG

Checking…

The Tapo bulbs and the LED strip are driven by LightRPG, which can run here or on the room computer. Set up and assign the bulbs and connect the strip on the LightRPG page once; the tracker then sends it a cue for each event below. Cues don't depend on a viewer's mute button. The LED strip always runs at full saturation and brightness: a Colour cue shows its hue on it, White and bulb effects show their LED colour (the ring around each swatch).

BETWEEN EVENTS

Ambient light

The lights go back to this after an event cue with a hold time. "Nothing" leaves them on the last cue.

NEXT MISSION

Mission outcome lights

Choose the colour or LightRPG effect used when a mission ends with the anomaly captured, killed or escaped. Each cue can return to the ambient light after its hold time.

TRACKER EVENTS

Event lights

"Hold" returns to the ambient light after that many seconds; "keep" leaves the cue on until the next one. Bulbs take a moment per change, so a new cue replaces one still waiting.

LOGIN SCREEN

Manager badge

The photo on the badge that drops in on its lanyard and goes through the card reader, cropped to a portrait frame. Without one the badge shows a silhouette. You can also drop a picture here.

LOGIN SCREEN

Sign-in timing

Seconds for each step, in the order they play after the click. A sign-in uses the values saved when it starts; the last one is the window closing on Log Out. With reduced motion turned on in the system, every step is kept short.

PORTABLE BACKUP

Settings file

Save sound assignments, volumes, delays, effects, lights, and login options in a separate file you can sync with your drive. Loading a file replaces the current settings. Uploaded sound files and the badge picture are not embedded in the JSON.
